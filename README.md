Client Portal & E-Commerce Storefront Overview

The AMK Clothing Client Portal is a dynamic, user-centric e-commerce and bespoke tailoring platform designed to provide a seamless and highly personalized shopping experience. 

Customers can browse curated ready-to-wear collections—spanning Women, Men, Curve, Kids, and Accessories—enhanced by integrated cart and wishlist functionalities, dynamic ZAR/USD currency conversion, and multi-language localization. 

Beyond traditional e-commerce, the platform features a robust Custom Orders module where clients can submit unique design requests, upload reference images, and either provide specific body measurements or schedule an in-person fitting. 

Through a secure, authenticated dashboard, users are empowered to track the live production status of their garments, seamlessly upload proof of payments, download detailed invoice slips, and communicate directly with the Johannesburg tailoring studio via an integrated support chat, ensuring a transparent, interactive, and premium custom fashion journey.
